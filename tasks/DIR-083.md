## Finding

Measured 2026-07-30: the two slowest individual tests in the suite are
dual-scan parity comparisons, both in
`internal/mcp/executor/fts_content_e2e_test.go`:
`TestQuerySessionContent_FTSParityWithCanonicalScan` (5.3s) and
`TestQuerySessionContent_UnicodeCaseFoldingParity` (4.3s) — together 9.6s of
serial wall time on every short run. They exist to prove the FTS/Unicode fast
path agrees with the canonical scan; that guarantee matters on the full path
(`make push` / CI), not on every `make commit` cycle.

Context: `go test -short -count=1 ./...` takes 46.3s total (executor alone
34.2s, 74%). `make commit` ≈62s; since 2026-07-28 there have been 79 acceptance
gate runs (≈82 min total wait, DIR-056 alone ran acceptance 9 times), so every
second removed from `-short` compounds across the whole gate history.
