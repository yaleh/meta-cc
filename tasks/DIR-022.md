## Finding

`internal/analysis/service.go:461-467` (`GetTechDebt`): when `source_dir` is provided and `ScanSourceDir` fails, the error is silently discarded behind `if scanErr == nil`. The comment now reads `// Degrade gracefully on scan error: keep the session-only result.` — graceful degradation is intended, but the error is neither logged nor returned, so users get session-only results with no indication the source scan was skipped.

## Proposal

Add an `slog.Warn` call on the scan-error path, including `source_dir` and the error, before returning the session-only result. Keep the graceful-degradation behavior (session-only result still returned). Optionally also surface the skip via the result's warnings if DIR-018's `Warnings` field has landed — do not block on DIR-018; the WARN log is the deliverable here.

## Plan

1. Add `slog.Warn("source scan failed; returning session-only result", "source_dir", sourceDir, "error", scanErr)` on the error branch.
2. Add a test that forces `ScanSourceDir` to fail and asserts the WARN log entry (capture via a test slog handler).
